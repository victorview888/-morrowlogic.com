Privacy policy

1. Information you share

If you contact us, you may choose to share your name, company, email address, phone number, partnership type, and message. Company and phone are optional.

2. Email drafts and form submissions

By default, the contact form prepares an email draft on your device; it does not send the form details to a server. You must open and send the draft yourself. If an online submission service is enabled, the form sends the details to that service over HTTPS.

3. Purpose and service providers

We use information you send to respond to your inquiry and follow up on that conversation. Website hosting, email, and any enabled form processing providers may handle information needed to provide those services.

4. Your choices

Share only information needed for your inquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent, contact us at the address below.

5. Browser storage and hosting

The site selects the language from the page URL. It does not add analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or store form details in browser storage. Hosting providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

6. Changes

We update this page when the way we handle information changes. Please review it before sending an inquiry.
